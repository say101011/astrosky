Privacy Policy

Astro Sky · Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains how Astro Sky uses information when you explore the sky, use the camera view, save preferences, or view advertising. Astro Sky does not require an account.

1. Information used for sky features

  • Location: For adult profiles, with your permission, the app obtains your device's available location through Android and Google Play services to calculate the sky for your observing position. You can instead enter latitude and longitude in Settings. Sky calculations use these coordinates locally. The app does not send them to an app-operated server or explicitly pass them to its advertising SDK. If you open the optional online location map, the displayed coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap as described in section 5.
  • Camera: Camera permission is used for the live augmented-reality background. The app does not record, save, or upload camera images or video.
  • Motion and compass sensors: Orientation, accelerometer, and magnetic-field readings align the sky display with your phone. The app processes these readings on the device and does not upload them.
  • Preferences: Favorites, display options, manual coordinates, and onboarding status are stored in the app's local storage. Search queries are used to search the bundled catalog; the app does not send them to a search service.

Astronomical calculations, catalog descriptions, and bundled photographs work offline. Android location services may operate according to your device's location settings and the relevant platform provider's policies.

2. Google advertising and consent

Astro Sky integrates Google AdMob banner advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app declares Android advertising-ID permission for adult advertising. For adult profiles, Google may use the advertising ID subject to applicable consent and device privacy settings. Under-18 and undisclosed-age profiles use CHILD treatment to prevent advertising-ID transmission. AdServices permissions remain removed. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses, permitted device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may indicate an approximate location. This is separate from the observing coordinates used by the sky map.

Advertising requests require an internet connection. For adult profiles, where applicable, the app asks for privacy choices through UMP before requesting live ads. Under-18 and undisclosed-age profiles use the child protection settings described in section 6. When required, you can revisit those choices under Settings → Google Privacy Options (Consent). Sample builds may display Google's test ads; test mode does not mean the SDK makes no network requests.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for further details.

3. Storage, backup, and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. This version disables Android app backup and excludes local app data from cloud backup and device transfer. Previously created backups remain subject to your device and account controls. Astro Sky does not operate its own account database or cloud-sync service.

You can remove individual favorites in the app. To remove local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Astro Sky → Storage → Clear data; wording varies by device. Clearing local storage does not itself delete information already processed by Google or remove a device backup. Those services have their own retention and deletion controls.

4. Your choices

  • Allow, deny, or revoke camera and location access in Android's app-permission settings.
  • Use manual coordinates if you prefer not to grant location permission. The sky map and catalog remain available without the camera.
  • Use Google Privacy Options in the app when available to review advertising consent.
  • Use the advertising-ID and privacy controls provided by Android and Google; availability varies by device and Android version.

5. External websites

Optional online location map: For adult profiles, Settings → View Location on Map displays an interactive OpenStreetMap map. After you confirm the disclosure and open this feature, the displayed device or manually selected coordinates are included in the request to OpenStreetMap. The map provider also receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, browser/app information, and the map areas you view. The map loads only while you use this feature; Astro Sky does not upload a location history or request location access from the map website. Map content may be cached on your device according to the provider's HTTP caching rules. The map website can also use web storage on your device for its operation and preferences; clearing the app's storage removes this local data. Read the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. You can use the offline astronomy features without opening this map.

In adult profiles, photo credits, educational sources, privacy information, and license links can open external websites. When you follow a link, that website may receive ordinary browser information, including your IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. This policy page has no analytics scripts or external fonts.

6. Children and personal information

Astro Sky serves children and older audiences. Before opening the app, a neutral age screen asks for an age group. Only an adult/non-adult flag is saved on this device; no birthday is collected or transmitted. After age selection, users under 18 and users who prefer not to state their age receive child-protected advertising: the app sets Google Mobile Ads age treatment to CHILD and maximum ad content rating to G before SDK initialization. UMP is separately marked as under the age of consent so it does not ask these users for advertising consent. Ad requests are made only when UMP permits them. This profile does not request device location, open online maps, or open external website links from the astronomy content. The app makes no ad requests before the age selection is completed. Camera previews, sensors, manual observing coordinates and the bundled catalog stay on the device. The app does not offer accounts, chat, purchases or user uploads. Advertising may process connection information, interactions, diagnostics and permitted identifiers as described above for service delivery and fraud prevention; child treatment restricts personalization and advertising identifiers. Ads may open the advertiser destination when tapped. Optional device location and online maps apply only to adult profiles. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact the developer using the contact method below.

7. Security and international processing

The app relies on Android's application sandbox to protect local data. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their policies. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

Changes to the app or its services may require updates to this policy. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

For questions about Astro Sky or this policy, contact say101011@gmail.com.
